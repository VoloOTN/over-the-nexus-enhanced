## Ruled Out

- **"The 2 in-memory mismatches (`eva.gy_stub`, `grapha.arm_stub`) are conversion errors."** Killed: the ORIGINAL
  unconverted functions also fail their golden rows (36 vs 40 B, 32 vs 36 B) because they post-process the `asm()` bytes.
  The converter now skips golden rows its own original does not reproduce.
- **Statically expanding macro helpers** (user decision): do not. Hand-port each helper into a function taking the builder.
- **Running the full `verify` sweep after every module.** Costs ~5 min 10 s - 5 min 30 s (as long as the build); the
  hash already proves the bytes. Use `verify <rom> --only cards.<module>` (2-6 s). Sweep once at the end (user, 2026-10-28).
- **Old-vs-new comparison for modules whose `_check` keys a RESERVE table by slot address** (ojamassimilation): calling a
  function at another function's base raises. Golden rows already cover each function at its own base; skip the compare.
- **Calling `main()`/`verify()` of a card module to compare before/after.** Both have no required args and rebuild and
  overwrite single-card ROMs in `data/over_the_nexus/working/`. Do not.
- **"cardcar_d should have been re-converted with the literal-call idiom."** It has no `ldr`+`blx` calls (its
  `ldr_const` loads data), so nothing to rewrite.
