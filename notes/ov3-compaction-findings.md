### Phase C task 2: fragment splitter (2026-10-06, session 2)
- `thumb_asm` gained two additive features, so no previously accepted source changes encoding:
  - `bl TARGET`: two-halfword ARMv4T BL, ±4 MB
  - `asm(..., symbols=)` and `labels_of(base, src)`, for labels outside the fragment
- `cave3.asm_split([(base, size, src), ...])` handles a stub laid across runs:
  - labels are global across fragments; each fragment gets its own literal pool
  - each fragment is checked for size, `assert_thumb1_only`, a source-level `pop {..lr}` reject
    (thumb_asm silently DROPS lr from a pop list), and a blob-level scan for BLX to an even
    literal
  - both negative controls fire: `blx` of literal 0x02000000, and `pop {r4,lr}`
- **Measured overhead** (scratchpad `split_overhead.py`): Eva `build_cost` (396 B), cut at `ph2:`
  across runs 0x021F0F9C (240 B) and 0x02212330 (244 B), 0x21394 apart, so `b` cannot reach:
  - fragments 228 + 184 = 412 B, **+16 B (4 %)**
  - that is 5 far BLs (+2 B each over a `b`) + 2 duplicated literals (+4 B each), less 2 B of
    alignment
  - rule of thumb: **+2 B per cross-fragment edge + 4 B per shared literal**
  - every BL target decoded to its intended label (disassembly read)
  - far BL is legal only after `push {..,lr}` (it clobbers LR)
- **thumb_asm change proven byte-neutral:** scratchpad `asm_equiv_build.py` wraps `asm` so that
  every call is also assembled by HEAD's thumb_asm (`git show`) and must match.
  - Result: 251 calls compared, 0 drift, build 101/101.
  - sha256 a2e96f76…5fe9437, which is identical to the known `rom_ov3_compact_chain.nds`.
